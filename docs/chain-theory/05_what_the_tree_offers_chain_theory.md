# What the Tree offers Chain Theory

Keyed to Chain Theory's box numbers and named open items. For each, the Tree result that looks
like a candidate, at the Tree's own grade, and what it would take to make it usable on the Chain
Theory side. Offered in the spirit Chain Theory itself asks for: "Corrections and objections are
welcome."

## Conjecture 0 · the three registers (box 1.7)

**Chain Theory's state.** That link ends have dimension two, three or four is Conjecture 0,
"graded a conditional theorem under its move table." The one-dimensional end is excluded at the
axiom (2.9); the upper end of the range, and the absence of 5, 6, 7, …, rests on the move table.

**The Tree's candidate.** The cascade (L4, GENUINE): End(M_b) ≅ M_{b²}, so from the seed M₂ the
levels are M₂, M₄, M₁₆, M₂₅₆, … — dimensions 2 and 4 are the first two, and *nothing of dimension
3, 5, 6, 7 is a level*. Colour (L13, GENUINE): the fundamental 4 of SU(4) splits as 3 ⊕ 1 under
the natural sub-algebra, so **3 appears as a derived sub-register of 4**, not as a level. The
Tree thus produces exactly the set {2, 4} as levels and {3} as a split of 4 — Chain Theory's
three registers — and *no others below 16*.

**What it would offer.** A generative basis for Conjecture 0: the registers are 2, 3, 4 because
2 is forced, 4 = End(2), and 3 is what 4 does when one direction is singled out. The next
candidate register would be 16, far above the read cap — which is consistent with Chain Theory
finding nothing above 4.

**What it would take.** Checking whether the Tree's End-iteration can be stated as (or
reproduces) Chain Theory's move table; and whether Chain Theory's networks ever require a
register that is not a cascade level or a split of one.

## Conjecture 2 · spacetime at the (2,2) cell (open result 1.9)

**Chain Theory's state.** "At the (2,2) cell the pair ket at the join has a zero office and no
acting center, and the derivation conjectures that the geometry of the compendium … is carried
by this cell." Beneath it, four gravity results stand open.

**The Tree's candidate.** L8 (GENUINE): Cl₄(ℂ) ≅ M₄(ℂ), and the Clifford algebra of
n-dimensional space is a full matrix algebra of the cascade's size *only* for n = 4. In words:
the two-qubit algebra *is* the Clifford algebra of four-dimensional spacetime, and no other
dimension's Clifford algebra is a level of the cascade.

**What it would offer.** Direct support for reading (2,2) as spacetime, from a theorem about the
object itself rather than from the gravity results above it. Chain Theory already notes "the
Clifford relation at four generators met" (ch. 3-5) and the spin-c group at the qubit pair
(58.1); the Tree's L8 says that this is not one option among several but the unique one.

**What it would take.** Nothing beyond reading L8's Lean file (`Cl4IsoM4`) against Chain Theory's
chapter 3-5. This is the easiest import in either direction.

## The missing number before the axiom · why anything?

**Chain Theory's state.** The axiom is the floor: "The only input."

**The Tree's candidate.** L1 (GENUINE): Lawvere's fixed-point theorem. In any cartesian closed
category with a point-surjective map A → (A → B), every endomorphism of B has a fixed point. Read
as the Tree reads it: a structure that can refer to itself cannot be empty of fixed points, so
*self-reference forces existence*. L2 (PARTIAL): the reflexive domain D ≃ (D → D) as the carrier.
L3 (GENUINE): the smallest non-commutative algebra that can host this is M₂(ℂ).

**What it would offer.** A step *below* the axiom: not "nothing exists but pairs of qudits" as a
posit, but "there must be a qubit" as a theorem, with the pairs then following as the natural
way for two qubits to be known of each other. Chain Theory takes Wheeler's "it from bit"
literally; the Tree offers a reason there is a bit.

**What it would take.** A translation of "maximally entangled pair, known by distilling" into
the language of the reflexive domain — in particular, whether Definition 1.6 ("known") is a
knowledge clause that D ≃ (D → D) already satisfies.

## The Weinberg angle · not found in the compendium

**Chain Theory's state.** A search of the compendium for "Weinberg angle", "weak mixing",
"sin²θ_W" and "3/8" finds no derived value (the Weinberg citations are to the soft-photon theorem,
the anthropic bound, and *A Model of Leptons*). The electroweak sector is reached through the
(4,2) cell "by name".

**The Tree's candidate.** L17 (PARTIAL): on the chiral 16 in exact rational arithmetic,
Tr(T₃L²)/Tr(Q²) = 3/8 and Tr(T₃L²)/Tr(Y²) = 3/5, with a control theorem showing a nearby
mechanism gives 3/7 — so the ratio is a property of the representation content, not of the
dimensions. Assumed: the coupling matching that turns the trace ratio into the physical angle at
unification.

**What it would offer.** A number for the (4,2) cell that lives on the same Pati–Salam object as
Chain Theory's gauge group. Chain Theory's W and Z are "one web, two reads" with "the ratio of
the two offices exactly ½" (164.5, conditional) and the W/Z mass ratio open (160.1); the Tree's
3/8 is the standard unification-scale value of the quantity that fixes M_W/M_Z. The two should
be read together.

**What it would take.** Identifying Chain Theory's "offices" at the W and Z webs with the
traces T₃L² and Q² on the Tree's chiral 16.

## The generation count · three generations as attach sites (197.5, open)

**Chain Theory's state.** Bound ≤ 3 is a theorem (197.3); three *is* the count is open (197.5):
"no lepton web of the ladder being isomorphic to a sink of the site-bounded census at wave four."

**The Tree's candidate.** L14 (POSTULATE, but with a specific mechanism): the real division
algebras are ℝ, ℂ, ℍ, 𝕆 (Frobenius / Hurwitz); ℍ embeds in M₂(ℂ) as the real form, and ℍ has
exactly three imaginary units. The Tree records "three generations are the imaginary
quaternions" as a postulate because the step from "three imaginary units" to "three copies of
the chiral 16" is a modelling choice.

**What it would offer.** A *candidate for the missing lower half*: Chain Theory shows ≤ 3; the
Tree proposes a structure inside the qubit itself with exactly three of something. If Chain
Theory's three "attach sites at the closed core triangle" can be matched to i, j, k, the open
result 197.5 and the Tree's postulate L14 would each be partially closed by the other.

**What it would take.** Checking whether the three core sites of Chain Theory's (3,3) triangle
carry a quaternionic structure, i.e. whether the three attach sites anticommute in the right way.

## The gravity programme · the four open results beneath Conjecture 2

**Chain Theory's state.** Curvature as graph Laplacian (90.2, conditional); field equation open;
"the universality of …" and three further results open.

**The Tree's candidate.** L18–L20 (PARTIAL): the spectral triple (A, H, D); the spectral action
Tr f(D/Λ) with *the exponential cutoff forced* — "the tensor-sum shape that the spectral
action's factorisation needs is forced precisely for one full matrix algebra at one generation,
and not otherwise" — and the Boltzmann measure of the action. L22 (OPEN): the Einstein equations
via the heat-kernel a₂ coefficient, gap G5, gated on G2.

**What it would offer.** The one route in the literature with a published derivation of the
Einstein–Hilbert action plus the full Standard Model Lagrangian from a single functional
(Chamseddine–Connes–Marcolli). Chain Theory cites it as a sibling [20]; it may also be a
*target*: if Chain Theory's graph-Laplacian curvature on the (2,2) ladder recovers the spectral
action's a₂ at any level, the open field equation would have a known continuum form to aim at.

**What it would take.** The joint computation in 06.

## The ungraded page · Table 239.1

**Chain Theory's state.** 74 open results at status open, 48 re-posed, 133 conditional clauses
"each a proposition with no proof term."

**The Tree's offer.** *The Staked Gaps* (10.5281/zenodo.23338726) does something Chain Theory's
register does not yet do: for each gap it states in advance what we expect to find when the gap
is closed, so that closing it is a test and not just a completion. The format may be useful for
the 74.
