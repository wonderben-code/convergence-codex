# A reconciliation map: Chain Theory and the Tree of Reality

**29 October 2026.** A working map of how the Tree of Reality (Mark E. Mala with Claude) relates to
A. D. Wissner-Gross's *Chain Theory* (23 October 2026), whose published work is cited throughout. Every
row cites the computation or proof behind it: results 08–13 in this folder, `scripts/chain_theory/` and
`lean_verify/chain_theory/`.

## The picture, stated as a hypothesis

The two programmes may be **one structure described from two ends**:
- **The Tree** describes *what there is*: an algebra that grows by applying End to itself (M₂ → M₄ →
  M₁₆ → …), with three lineages, End (the thing), Aut (its symmetries) and ⟨·,·⟩ (how it is known).
- **Chain Theory** describes *what can be known*: networks of maximally entangled pairs and the reads
  that fix them, built from three primitives, the thing (pairs of qudits), the matching (basis
  identifications) and the known (what all reads fix).

The results of the last four days make this concrete in places. They do not prove it.

## The one open tension

Chain Theory's axiom makes every link maximally entangled. In the Tree's bidoublet reading, the
physical Higgs vacuum is **almost a product state** (about 0.002 bits, R14). A maximally entangled
vacuum would force m_t = m_b (Lean). Chain Theory assigns quark masses through the offices of
separate webs, which may be its way past this. It is recorded as an open question, not a claim.

## Tests that would decide the hypothesis

1. **Networks from standard forms.** Tensor GNS standard forms together and see whether Chain
   Theory's networks arise, with the third copy supplied by the tensor product (R6's open half).
2. **The exchanges.** Find the operation on the Tree's side that reproduces the Letter's X and Y,
   with their signs. If one exists, the 172-record space, and with it the α number's numerator
   structure, is built from the Tree's lineages.
3. **The moves.** Check whether Chain Theory's move table is generated by cascade operations (Q10's
   open half; the L11 choice).
4. **The generations.** Settle whether CP violation is explicit or spontaneous (R15's discriminant),
   the one place where the two readings of "three" make different predictions.
