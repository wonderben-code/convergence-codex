# What Chain Theory offers the Tree

Keyed to the Tree's link and gap codes. For each open item in the Tree, the Chain Theory result
that looks like a candidate, at Chain Theory's own grade, and what it would take to import it.

## L23 · Yang–Mills and the mass gap — OPEN (walls W1–W4)

**The Tree's state.** A precisely stated wall with no covering theorem; gaps G1 (infinite-volume
limit), G3 (Peierls comparison), G4 (Dobrushin/cluster) sit behind it.

**Chain Theory's candidate.** Confinement as *dark-genre readability* (171.1, conditional):
the (3,3) link has d₁d₂ = 9 > 6, so by the read cap no single distillation fixes both ends — "at
all 494 qutrit–qutrit links of the roster, no read survives at first order and the second-order
read carries the readout." The mechanism is the Størmer–Woronowicz–Choi classification of
positive maps, the same threshold at which bound entanglement first appears. The strong coupling
is then the gluon closed read (172.1, conditional); a hadron is "a star: a junction qutrit of
degree three carrying three quark bodies."

**What it would offer.** A *reason* for confinement that is finite, exact, and about the colour
algebra itself, rather than an analytic estimate on a lattice. The Tree's colour triplet (L13) is
exactly a qutrit, so the (3,3) cell is M₃ ⊗ M₃ inside the colour block of M₁₆. The read cap
would say: in the Tree's own object, no single "knowing" fixes two colour ends.

**What it would take.** Translating "read" into the Tree's inner-product lineage (a conditional
expectation in a MUB), then checking whether the decomposability threshold survives in the
spectral-triple setting. This would not close G1/G3/G4 — those are about the continuum limit —
but it might reclassify L23 from "wall" to "mechanism stated, continuum limit open."

## L14 · Three generations — POSTULATE

**The Tree's state.** "Three generations are the imaginary quaternions" is recorded as a
physical postulate (entry 25 of the assumptions ledger); the mathematics proves what follows
from it, not the postulate itself.

**Chain Theory's candidate.** Two results that bracket the number from above:
- **Generation bound at the hub dimension (197.3, theorem):** "a hub assembly is a ququart
  carrying a qubit pair among its qubit legs, the pair takes two of its four ends and every move
  applies at a site with spare alone, so a hub assembly admits at most the third generation at
  the hub dimension four."
- **Three, a theorem of the valence (83.1):** at the three core sites of the closed (3,3)
  triangle, the rungs terminate at three "at every lepton seed."
And one honest open item: **three generations as attach sites (197.5, open)** — "why the Part
calls the third a reading rather than a theorem."

**What it would offer.** The Tree's hub is M₄ = M₂ ⊗ M₂ (L4). If Chain Theory's counting
argument transfers, the Tree gets a theorem-grade **upper bound** of three on generations from
its own first cascade step — something it does not have. Combined with the Tree's quaternionic
*candidate reason* for exactly three (ℍ ⊂ M₂(ℂ) has three imaginary units), the two halves might
meet: Chain Theory says "no more than three"; the Tree says "here is a three". L14 could move
from POSTULATE to "bounded above by theorem; identification of the three with the imaginary
quaternions open."

**What it would take.** Stating the hub-assembly argument in the language of M₄: what are the
"legs", what is "spare", and is the bound a statement about tensor factorisations of M₄ that can
be proved in Mathlib.

## L22 · The Einstein equations from the spectral action — OPEN (gap G5, gated on G2)

**The Tree's state.** The heat-kernel expansion of Tr f(D/Λ) with a₂ identified as a curvature
integral requires unbounded self-adjoint operators (G2), which the Lean estate does not yet have.
The route is the Chamseddine–Connes one.

**Chain Theory's candidate.** A combinatorial route that never passes through an unbounded
operator: depth metric with the rung term (90.1, conditional); **curvature as the graph Laplacian
washed over the ladder (90.2, conditional)**; horizon at every rung as a seeded saturation
(99.1, conditional); abundances and the rate at the horizon (104.1, theorem); the cosmological
term at the depth exponent (115.1, conditional). Chain Theory is clear that "the four results of
the gravity program beneath the conjecture stand as open results" and the field equation is open.

**What it would offer.** A finite, exact *stand-in* for curvature at each level of the cascade,
computable now, while G2 is worked on. Whether the graph Laplacian on the cascade's dependency
structure recovers the a₂ coefficient in any limit is a real question (see 06), but even a
negative answer would sharpen what G5 needs.

**What it would take.** Defining Chain Theory's "depth" and "rung" on the Tree's levels (depth n
↔ M_{2^{2^n}} is the natural guess), then computing the graph Laplacian of the induced ladder.

## L21 · Hilbert space, GNS, Stone, Schrödinger — PARTIAL (the Born rule as cited input)

**The Tree's state.** The Born rule, Gleason and Wigner are "cited physics, not theorems here"
(entry 59).

**Chain Theory's candidate.** The Born rule as an output of the wash: the state space of a pair
type as the span of act outputs (35.1, conditional) — "the inner product of the span is the Born
readout"; the flat pair and unitary transport (40.1, conditional). Chain Theory notes the
premises: "conditional on the axiom text and on two imports of its parent result, the Hilbert
space of a qudit and the pair as a ray."

**What it would offer.** A different *ordering*: Chain Theory takes the Hilbert space of a qudit
and derives the probability rule; the Tree takes the inner product (its third lineage) and
derives the Hilbert space by GNS, citing the probability rule. Reading the two side by side might
show that between them the circle closes — the Tree's GNS supplies Chain Theory's "Hilbert space
of a qudit" import, and Chain Theory's wash supplies the Tree's cited Born rule.

## L9 · Lorentzian signature — PARTIAL

**The Tree's state.** A fork: the signature is reached, but the step that excludes the Euclidean
alternative is not fully proved.

**Chain Theory's candidate.** The Lorentz group as the isometries of the Hermitian split (54.1,
conditional): "a form of signature one against three, is six-dimensional and equals the Lorentz
algebra at every structure constant"; one against three at every step of the chain (55.1,
theorem); and a lattice argument — the indefinite form t² − 2x² admits infinitely many integer
isometries (a Pell equation) while a definite form admits finitely many, so "the signature by
existence reads Lorentzian at all eight ratios."

**What it would offer.** A new kind of argument for the Tree's fork: not that Lorentzian
signature is *consistent*, but that only the indefinite form has an infinite symmetry group on
the lattice of counts. If the Tree's levels carry a natural lattice (they do: the integer
structure of M₄'s Clifford generators), the Pell argument may be stateable there.

## L15 · The Higgs sector and its coupling window — PARTIAL

**The Tree's state.** A proven window for the Higgs coupling, not a value.

**Chain Theory's candidate.** The Higgs quartic as the ratio of offices (202.1, theorem): "an
exact element of ℚ(√2, √3) at every one of 10,123 Higgs-form sinks."

**What it would offer.** A point to test against the window. If Chain Theory's exact quartic
lies inside the Tree's window, each supports the other; if outside, one of the two has learned
something specific. This is the cheapest test in this folder.

## L12 · Chirality — POSTULATE

**Chain Theory's candidate.** Parity as the improper element (59.1, conditional); the three
involutions and the identity product — end swap, chain reversal, and the third, with CPT as their
product (61.1, theorem).

**What it would offer.** A structural account of what "left" means that does not require
choosing a factor by hand: the improper element of the Hermitian split's isometry group. Whether
that singles out one factor of M₄ ⊗ M₄ is open.

## L24 · The numbers — PARTIAL

**The Tree's state.** Honest: "Everything else — Newton's constant, the cosmological constant,
fermion masses, proton lifetime — is not computed." The two things computed are the Weinberg
ratio 3/8 and the Higgs window.

**Chain Theory's candidate.** The α count: (79 + 48√2 + 42√3 + 3√6)/31104 as a theorem of the
definitions, conjectured to be α(0) (Conjecture 1). Also: the electron anomalous moment through
third order, the hydrogen spectrum, the running of the couplings, decay rates as finite sums
(all conditional); the muon/electron mass ratio (conditional at both faces); the W/Z office ratio
exactly ½ (164.5, conditional).

**What it would offer.** A worked example of how a *number* comes out of a finite construction:
count records, weight by exchange eigenvalues, divide by the readout dimension. The Tree has the
object (the 3 ⊗ 2 block of M₁₆ after 4 → 3⊕1 is a qutrit–qubit pair) but has never asked it
for a number. Running Chain Theory's count on the Tree's object is the first joint experiment
in 06.

## G6 · the isotypic bimodule and K-theory — the CCM algebra

**Chain Theory's candidate.** Nothing direct — Chain Theory does not use the spectral triple.
But its particle webs (16 webs for 17 particles, with each generation adding one hub assembly)
are an explicit finite model of the fermion content that CCM's H_F encodes abstractly. A
dictionary between the webs and the basis of H_F would be useful to both.
