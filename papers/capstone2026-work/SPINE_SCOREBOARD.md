## What the tags mean

The vocabulary is the one `SPINE_CAMPAIGN.md` §4 fixed on 14 September, applied to each link's **published headline**:

* **GENUINE** — the headline is a machine-checked theorem statement: no `sorry`, standard axioms only (`propext`, `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound`), and the statement says what the headline says.
* **PARTIAL** — real theorems cover part of the headline, and the rest is stated as missing.
* **POSTULATE** — the headline is a modelling choice, recorded in the assumptions ledger, not a theorem-shaped statement.
* **OPEN** — a precisely stated wall in the walls ledger, with no covering theorem.

**Three tags were asked for; the table has four, and this file keeps the fourth.** Folding a PARTIAL link into GENUINE would tag it up. Read strictly three ways, the count is **6 GENUINE and 18 not**; each PARTIAL link's residue is named in its row below, and it is either open mathematics or a postulate.

**The rule applied without exception:** no postulate is rated as a theorem because a theorem stands near it. Where a headline is machine-checked **false** as written — L2's non-trivial `D ≃ (D → D)` over sets, L9's *(4,0) and (0,4) excluded structurally*, L10's embedding into `su(4)` — the link is rated on what real theorems cover, and the row names the refuted sentence.

## What the scoreboard does not say

* **It is not a verdict on the physics.** A GENUINE link is a theorem about the estate's objects; whether those objects are the physical ones is, link by link, the modelling choice the assumptions ledger records.
* **The two OPEN links are the two real walls**: L22 (the Einstein equations from the spectral action, `WALLS` W5) and L23 (Yang–Mills and the mass gap, `WALLS` W1–W4).
* **No rating moved in units 75–243.** Five recompute addenda in `SPINE.md` (20, 25 and 26 September) read units 75–234 against the table and each found no rating move, and every later unit that touched a link recorded *rating unchanged*. The work sharpened residues, closed named sub-gaps and filed errata; the headline tags stood each time they were re-checked.
* **Three numberings share the word *decision*.** *DECISIONS NEEDED n* is item n of the running list in `PROGRESS_LOG.md` (items 1–30); *DECISION 5* and *DECISION 6* are the early-August series, now items 20 and 21 of that list; *ASSUMPTIONS n* is entry n of the assumptions ledger (`ERRATUM 683`).
